FAQ

Questions about toasting.

What is the SOL I get back?

Solana charges a refundable deposit, called rent, for every token account. That is about 0.00204 SOL for each coin you have ever held. Close the account and the deposit comes back to you.

Does TOAST take a fee?

No. You only pay the normal Solana network fee, a fraction of a cent for each transaction.

Is it safe?

Your wallet signs every transaction and you can check each one before you sign. TOAST only burns pump.fun and bonk coins, never LP tokens or NFTs, and refuses anything worth more than $25.

What if a dead coin comes back?

Burning is permanent. Untick anything you still believe in before you toast.

Why does my wallet need a little SOL?

The network fee is paid before the rent comes back, so the wallet needs about 0.0001 SOL to start.

Which wallets work?

Phantom, Solflare, Backpack and any wallet that supports Wallet Standard. On a phone, open this site inside your wallet’s browser.
